Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Chat with us on WhatsApp — we reply within minutes.

Ask on WhatsApp
How do I book a seat?▼
Just click "Book Now" on any trip card on this page. It will open WhatsApp with your chosen trip details pre-filled. Send that message, confirm with us, pay via UPI or cash, and your seat is confirmed! The whole process takes less than 5 minutes.
Is the price per person or for the whole group?▼
The price shown on every trip card is per seat / per person. For example, if a trip shows ₹699, that is ₹699 per traveller. Groups of 10 or more can ask for special group pricing — just mention your group size on WhatsApp.
What happens if the trip gets cancelled?▼
In the rare event we cancel a trip (due to weather, low bookings, or emergency), every passenger gets a full 100% refund within 48 hours via the same payment method. We always notify the trip WhatsApp group at least 3 days in advance if possible.
Is food included in the trip price?▼
It depends on the trip. Some trips include breakfast, some include hotel + meals, and some are transport-only. Check the "What's Included" tags on each trip card for exact details. We always mention this clearly before you confirm your booking.
Can I book multiple seats for family or friends?▼
Yes, absolutely! You can book as many seats as are available. Simply tell us the number of seats you need on WhatsApp. We will hold the seats for 24 hours after confirmation, during which you can arrange payment. For large groups (10+), special arrangements can be made.
From which cities do trips depart?▼
Most of our trips depart from cities in Western and Central Uttar Pradesh — including Jahangirabad, Bulandshahr, Aligarh, Moradabad, Sambhal, Badaun, and surrounding areas. Each trip card clearly mentions the departure city. Contact us if you need pickup from a specific location.
Are the buses AC or non-AC?▼
We operate both AC and non-AC buses. The type is mentioned in each trip's details. For hill destinations and summer trips, we prefer AC buses. For short religious trips, non-AC sleeper or semi-sleeper coaches are typically used. All buses are well-maintained and sanitized.
